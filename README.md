Harbor Master is a harbour traffic game: guide every ship to its matching coloured dock. The right dock means a fast unload, full points and a growing combo; the wrong dock is slow and pays half. Ships that bump into each other cost a strike — three strikes and the harbour closes. VIP liners pay a +300 bonus if docked within 30 seconds, rush hours send three ships at once, and fog rolls in from level 3. Every level opens a free upgrade shop — faster cranes, extra hull, a tugboat that guides a ship for you, or a Golden Anchor that saves your combo from a wrong dock. Every 5th level the Storm Galleon sails in: a huge boss ship worth 500 points if you dock it before its 45-second clock runs out. Four ship types, rising traffic, procedural WebAudio sound, mouse and keyboard controls. Plain canvas and vanilla JavaScript, no assets, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click a ship, then click its matching coloured dock (or press keys 1–4). Space pauses, R restarts, M mutes.
